## First validation cycle
Time box: seven days after access and outreach approval.
Planning targets, not forecasts:
1. Identify 12 candidate businesses across at least two accessible niches.
2. Seek five substantive asynchronous owner responses.
3. Get two anonymized walkthroughs of the existing lead process.
4. Obtain one written pilot agreement with a price and measurement plan.

No outreach has been sent. No prospect list has been collected.

## Owner discovery questions
- How many inbound inquiries did you receive in the last month, by channel?
- Which were unanswered or left without a next step? Can you show anonymized counts?
- Who handles them now, and what tools do you already pay for?
- What happens when your team follows up manually?
- What is typical contribution after direct job costs, not just invoice value?
- What have you tried? Why did it fail or remain unused?
- What customer situations must always go to your staff?
- Would you permit a narrow pilot, share outcome data, and pay a specified amount if the agreed conditions hold?

Collect counts and workflow descriptions first, not personal customer data.

## Selection rule
Select a niche only when an accessible owner has:
- a demonstrated recurring gap;
- enough eligible leads to observe the process;
- an unmet need beyond existing tools;
- authority to approve a pilot;
- a credible positive contribution scenario and willingness to pay.

If an existing tool meets the need, consider paid configuration/managed operations rather than building a duplicate.
If five substantive discussions find no painful paid problem, revise the offer before building.
If response is low, investigate access/channel quality before declaring the market invalid.

## Economics
Expected recovered contribution = eligible leads × incremental completion rate × contribution per completed job.
Net customer benefit = recovered contribution - Oto fee - additional customer operating costs.
Oto contribution = collected Oto fees - provider costs - support/delivery labor costs.
Use low/base/high scenarios with owner-supplied inputs. Count founder time.
No numerical price is validated yet.

## Outreach guardrail
Canadian commercial electronic messages generally require consent, sender identification, and unsubscribe handling. Publicly listed contact details are not blanket permission.
Source: https://crtc.gc.ca/eng/com500/guide.htm
Document a valid basis and have the applicable workflow reviewed before live messaging. This is a launch gate, not legal advice.
